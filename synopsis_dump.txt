[22] [Style=normal] (sb=177800, sa=50800, ls=None): 1.1 Purpose of the Project
[23] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): In modern global capital markets, retail and institutional market participants operate in an environment characterized by extreme data fragmentation, high-frequency price volatility, latent execution risks, and severe information asymmetry. Traditional retail web portals and financial tools frequently exhibit three critical systemic vulnerabilities:
[24] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): 1. Data Latency & Stale Feeds: Most free-tier financial applications rely on delayed 15-minute exchange updates or non-deterministic scraping routines. During sessions of heightened volatility, retail traders are forced to make capital allocation decisions based on obsolete market quotes, leading to execution slippage and unhedged drawdowns.
[25] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): 2. Numerical String-Formatting Corruption: A widespread software defect in front-end financial interfaces arises when numerical price strings containing formatting delimiters (such as commas) are improperly evaluated. For example, executing standard floating-point conversion routines such as JavaScript `parseFloat("24,440.05")` causes the parser to truncate at the first non-numeric character (the comma), incorrectly returning a corrupted price of 24.0. In downstream trading interfaces, secondary intervals multiplying against this distorted baseline distort index values to corrupted numbers (e.g. Nifty 50 jumping to 249.96), creating catastrophic user error.
[26] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): 3. Absence of Non-Linear Causal Risk Analytics: When a macroeconomic or geopolitical shock occurs—such as naval conflict escalations in key shipping straits or localized weather disruptions in agricultural belts—retail investors lack analytical frameworks to determine how first-order commodity price spikes ripple into second-order corporate margin compression and third-order systemic equity drawdowns. Traditional stock screeners evaluate assets in linear isolation, failing to model multi-order economic interdependencies.
[27] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): Project VITTIYA was engineered to directly overcome these structural challenges. VITTIYA is a full-stack, high-frequency financial market terminal, Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) Causal Risk Engine, Chittorgarh real-time verified IPO tracking platform, and Generative Financial Reasoner. The system connects to Yahoo Finance v8 REST API endpoints via a resilient 8-second polling stream, executes regular-expression numerical sanitization before floating-point conversion, tracks ongoing and upcoming primary market IPOs with live Grey Market Premiums (GMP), and provides non-linear risk simulation capabilities.

[52] [Style=normal] (sb=127000, sa=25400, ls=None): 2.1.1 Topological Sort Causal DAG Propagation Algorithm
[53] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): To model how macroeconomic shocks propagate through interconnected asset classes without infinite feedback loops, VITTIYA models the market network as a Directed Acyclic Graph G = (V, E), where V represents financial nodes (events, commodities, industries, portfolio holdings) and E represents directional causal relationships. The impact score I_u for any target node u in V is calculated via topological propagation:
[54] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): Equation 2.1: I_u = SUM_{v in Parent(u)} ( I_v * Weight_{v -> u} * Confidence_v * Severity_trigger )
[55] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): Where I_v is the incoming shock value of parent node v, Weight_{v -> u} is the structural coupling weight between node v and u, Confidence_v is the confidence probability score of the source event, and Severity_trigger is the normalized magnitude of the primary macro event.
[56] [Style=normal] (sb=127000, sa=25400, ls=None): 2.1.2 Sanitize-and-Parse (SAP) Numerical Engine
[57] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): To eliminate price distortion bugs caused by localized string formatting (e.g. commas in numerical strings), incoming API payloads are processed through a strict regular expression sanitization pipeline before IEEE 754 floating-point evaluation:
[58] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): Equation 2.2: P_clean = parseFloat( P_raw.toString().replace(/,/g, "").replace(/[^0-9.]/g, "") )
[59] [Style=normal] (sb=127000, sa=25400, ls=None): 2.1.3 Dynamic IST Market Operating Clock Algorithm
[60] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): The Indian Standard Time (IST = UTC + 5:30) operating clock dynamically determines whether the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) are actively trading. It evaluates UTC timestamp offsets against the trading window (09:15 AM to 03:30 PM IST, Monday through Friday), automatically toggling the UI header badge between "NSE / BSE LIVE" and "NSE CLOSED (Opens 09:15 AM IST)".
[61] [Style=normal] (sb=127000, sa=25400, ls=None): 2.1.4 Grey Market Premium (GMP) & Estimated Listing Return Projection Engine
[62] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): The estimated listing price P_listing and estimated listing gain percentage R_listing for primary market IPO applications are dynamically computed using:
[63] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): Equation 2.3: P_listing = P_upper_cap + GMP_absolute
[64] [Style=normal] (sb=None, sa=63500, ls=1.15): Equation 2.4: R_listing = ( GMP_absolute / P_upper_cap ) * 100%
